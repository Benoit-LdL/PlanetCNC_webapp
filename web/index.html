Tool in the 3D view

The tool size and shape are read from the tool comments this CAM program writes at the top of the G-code file. When a file has no tool comments, the diameter TNG reports for the loaded tool is used if it has one. Turned off, the standard tool marker is shown.

Telegram

Messages about jobs on your phone, with camera photos and the timelapse, from your own Telegram bot. It only reports: it can't control the machine.

Set up: in Telegram, talk to @BotFather, send /newbot and paste the token it gives here. Then send /start to your new bot, press Refresh and choose your chat.

    Timelapse

    The camera keeps running during the job. At the end the photos become a short video (about 20 s), sent to Telegram; the last 20 stay on the Pi. Long jobs take photos less often as they go.

      Software and system

      Versions, updates of this app and of Raspberry Pi OS, and rebooting the Pi are in the System window.

      Logs

      Writes much more (every request), for chasing a problem. Turn it off afterwards.
